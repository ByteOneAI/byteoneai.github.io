This Privacy Policy explains how ByteOneAI ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit our website or use our products, including Matridata, Nayam360, our Real Estate CRM and Store Builder for Shopify. We process personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Who we are

ByteOneAI is the brand of ByteOne AI, a software business based in Bengaluru, India, at Seegehalli, Kadugodi, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560067.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company name and the content of messages you send through our contact forms, email or WhatsApp, and account details when you sign up for a product.

Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages visited and how you use our website and apps, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

Payment information: where a product is paid, payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card details.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve our website and products
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • To send service messages, such as account and security notices
  • To send product updates where you have agreed to receive them; you can opt out at any time
  • To keep our services secure and prevent misuse
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

4. Data in our products

Some of our products are used by businesses to manage their own customers' data — for example, a clinic using Nayam360 to manage patient appointments and prescriptions, a real estate company using our CRM to manage leads, or a merchant using Store Builder with their Shopify store. In those cases the business decides how that data is used, and we process it only on their behalf and under their instructions.

Some features use AI models to generate or analyse messages. Data sent to these models is used only to provide the feature and is handled under our agreements with those providers.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our products, such as cloud hosting, messaging (including the WhatsApp Business Platform), AI model providers, analytics and payment processors
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • A successor business, if ByteOneAI is involved in a merger or acquisition

Some providers may store data outside India. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure it stays protected as required by law.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it
  • Ask us to erase it
  • Withdraw consent you have given, at any time
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Our website and products are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data without verifiable parental consent.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. For significant changes we will notify you through our website or products.
