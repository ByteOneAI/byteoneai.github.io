About us

We build software that earns its place in your day.

Our story

ByteOneAI grew out of more than a decade spent building enterprise software for large organisations, including some of the most demanding systems in banking. Again and again we saw teams lose hours to work that good software could simply handle for them.

So we started building that software ourselves — for clinics juggling appointments on paper, for real estate teams losing leads to slow follow-ups, and for people who just want an app that works.

ByteOneAI is a small, independent studio based in Bengaluru. That keeps us close to the people who use what we make, and lets us move quickly when they need something to change.
